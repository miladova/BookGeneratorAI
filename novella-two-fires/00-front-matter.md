# TWO FIRES

### A Novella of Poland, 1958

---

> *A village has two fires: the one in the hearth and the one in the church. A woman's life is carrying a coal from one to the other without dropping it.*
>
> — saying attributed to Marianna Kędzierska (1893–1944)

---

*For everyone who carried a coal.*

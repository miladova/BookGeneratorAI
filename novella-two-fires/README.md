# Two Fires — a novella of Poland, 1958

**A complete 10-chapter historical novella (~48,000 words).**

Warsaw, October 1958. Zofia Kędzierska, Deputy Director of the Office for Religious
Affairs, opens a file on the three men proposed for the vacant See of Sandomierz. The
third name is Aleksander Zaremba: thirty-six years old, a parish priest in Żoliborz, a
former Home Army soldier, three years in Rakowiecka prison — and the boy she loved in a
village in the Kielce hills in the summer of 1938.

## Structure

1. **The Dossier** — Warsaw, October 1958
2. **Two Fires** — Wierzbica, 1938
3. **The Machine and the Willow** — Wierzbica, 1939–1944
4. **What the Living Do** — Kielce, 1945
5. **The Plank** — Kraków, 1945–1950
6. **Room 214** — Warsaw, 9 October 1958
7. **The File** — Warsaw and Wierzbica, October 1958
8. **The Empty Church** — Warsaw, 26 October 1958
9. **The Opinion** — Warsaw, 27–30 October 1958
10. **The Second Fire** — Warsaw, 1958–1959

Alternating close-third between Zofia and Aleksander; chapters 5, 6 and 8 are his,
the rest are hers.

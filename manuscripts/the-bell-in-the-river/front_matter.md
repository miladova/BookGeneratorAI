**A note on names and money**

Polish names are given as the characters used them. In 1938 a village outside
Mława spoke of *ojciec* and *matka*, of *pan* and *pani*, and used the diminutives
that no official document ever recorded: Zosia, Róża, and, in one household only,
*Zosieczka*. The narrator has kept them. A word about the surname Wierzbicka: the
Office's files spell it correctly. Most of the people in this book do not.

Money is in złoty. In 1938 a farm labourer earned about sixty złoty a month; a
pair of good boots cost fifteen; a cow, three hundred. In 1958 a teacher earned
eleven hundred a month and a decent coat cost nine hundred. The reader may find
these figures useful; the people in this book certainly did.
